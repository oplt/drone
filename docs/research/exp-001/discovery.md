# EXP-001 discovery — multispectral & prescription readiness

## Named customer problem

Early-season canopy stress and treatment handoff for mid-size arable farms that
already fly RGB survey missions and want **calibrated vegetation indices** and
**machine-readable treatment maps** without trusting unverified NDVI or rate
advice.

Primary pain today: RGB crop-health detections are reviewable findings, not
calibrated stress maps; competitors advertise NDVI/prescription exports that
this product must not claim until calibration, approval, and consumer load
tests pass.

## Supported hardware shortlist (research target)

| Sensor class | Candidate models | Required extras |
|---|---|---|
| Multispectral | MicaSense RedEdge-P / Altum-PT; DJI Mavic 3M | Reflectance panel captures; downwelling irradiance (DLS) when available |
| Thermal (adjacent) | Altum-PT LWIR; FLIR Vue | Radiometric calibration artifact |
| Application equipment (export consumer) | ISO 11783 Task Controller / John Deere Operations Center shapefile import | Documented CRS, zone attributes, **no chemical rates** until regulatory ownership |

RGB/video remains the audited production capture path. Multispectral capture may
be inventoried in mission profiles for planning only.

## Index formulas in scope

- **NDVI** = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)
- **GNDVI** = (NIR − Green) / (NIR + Green)
- NDRE/SAVI explicitly **out of research scope** until a second fixture set exists

Coordinate system for research exports: EPSG:4326 for review GeoJSON/shapefile;
machine application CRS deferred to consumer discovery (often projected local).

## Regulatory / agronomy review notes

- Prescription drafts must stay `inspection_only` unless an approved agronomy
  rule carries a regulatory reference and human approval.
- No fertilizer/chemical rates are generated by the research path.
- Agronomist sign-off is mandatory before any external export of zones.

## Explicit non-claims

Until ADR-003 is GO and a separate production task set is approved:

- No production capability id for multispectral stress or treatment maps
- No marketing claim that NDVI/prescription outputs are generally available
- Shapefile research export ≠ validated ISOXML machine contract
