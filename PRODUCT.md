# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Farmers, agronomists, drone operators, and operations teams working outdoors, in vehicles, and at desks. They need to plan repeatable field flights, verify capture quality, review geospatial evidence, compare crop changes, and approve safe follow-up actions.

## Product Purpose

Turn agricultural drone flights into traceable, georeferenced observations that answer what changed, where it happened, how severe and certain it is, and what requires physical inspection. Success means trustworthy evidence and recoverable workflows, not visually impressive but unsupported diagnoses.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, trustworthy. The interface should feel operational and evidence-led, with direct language suitable for time-sensitive field work.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative AI dashboards, color-only status communication, tiny dense controls, opaque model claims, raw detection-box-first workflows, gratuitous motion, and interfaces that assume perfect connectivity or desktop-only use.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before claims: show lineage, confidence, uncertainty, and review state.
- Field-ready clarity: make primary tasks usable in sunlight, on touch devices, and under poor connectivity.
- Geospatial first: map observations and affected area; keep raw frames as supporting evidence.
- Safe action boundary: separate observation, recommendation, human approval, and export.
- Honest recovery: expose missing calibration, telemetry gaps, partial runs, and retry paths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, strong visible focus, semantic status announcements, color-independent severity and trend labels, reduced motion, 44-by-44-pixel touch targets, responsive reflow, text zoom, and screen-reader-readable maps and evidence.
